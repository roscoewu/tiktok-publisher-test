Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar and open ID
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to publish videos you confirm
  • Publishing status of videos sent through the tool

How we use it

The data is used only to show which account you are posting to and to publish the videos you confirm. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored encrypted on the tool's server and are deleted when you disconnect your account or when testing ends. Uploaded videos are deleted from the tool's storage after publishing.

Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, or ask us to delete your data via the contact below.
